🚗 Car Rental Services

Complete Car Rental Management for Odoo 19

A full-featured car rental solution for agencies, corporate fleets, and vehicle leasing businesses. Manage your entire rental operation — from service types and fleet to bookings and customer history — all inside Odoo.

Key Features

📋 Service Types

Define and manage rental service categories — Economy, Standard, Premium, Luxury, SUV, Chauffeur, Electric, Van, and more. Each type carries its own pricing tiers (daily, weekend, weekly, monthly), kilometre policy, inclusions (driver, fuel, insurance, GPS), and vehicle class.

🚙 Fleet Management

Track every vehicle in your fleet: license plate, VIN, brand, fuel type, transmission, odometer, insurance expiry, registration expiry, and maintenance schedule. Real-time status badges (Available, Rented, Reserved, Maintenance) keep your team informed.

📅 Booking Management

Create, confirm, and track customer bookings with auto-generated references. Manage the full rental lifecycle: Draft → Confirmed → Ongoing → Returned. The built-in calendar view gives you a visual overview of all active rentals.

💰 Flexible Pricing

Set base daily, weekend, weekly, and monthly rates per service type. Configure security deposits, extra kilometre charges, and free-km allowances. Pricing auto-populates on bookings based on the chosen service type.

What's Included

  • ✅ 8 pre-configured service types (Economy, Standard, Premium, Luxury, SUV, Chauffeur, Electric, Van)
  • ✅ Full CRUD for Service Types, Vehicles, and Bookings
  • ✅ List, Kanban, Form, and Calendar views
  • ✅ Smart buttons — jump from service type → vehicles → bookings
  • ✅ Customer history — rental count visible on partner form
  • ✅ Chatter & activity tracking on all major records
  • ✅ Auto booking reference sequence (CRB/YYYY/XXXXX)
  • ✅ Odoo 19 compatible

Installation

  1. Copy the car_rental_services folder into your Odoo addons directory.
  2. Restart the Odoo server.
  3. Go to Apps → Update Apps List.
  4. Search for Car Rental Services and click Install.

The module installs with 8 default service types ready to use immediately.
